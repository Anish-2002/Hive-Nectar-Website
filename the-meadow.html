The Meadow

Built for the space between corporate sustainability and everyday behaviour.


Work, family, kids, chores, deadlines, responsibilities. These endless loops exhaust us, making it hard to keep up, stay calm, and recognise ourselves.


The Meadow is a living system, designed around small, low-effort daily activities and gentle prompts that help strengthen meaningful attachment to family, values, identity, community, place, and yourself.
