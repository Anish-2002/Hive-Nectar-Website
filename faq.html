Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about The Meadow

Getting Started

What is The Meadow?
The Meadow is a gamified sustainability platform that helps you build positive habits through daily micro-tasks. Complete tasks, earn Nectar points, level up, and join a community of changemakers working together for a better future.
How do I create an account?
Simply click "Join the Meadow" and fill out the signup form with your name, email, phone, and address. You'll receive a verification code to confirm your account, and then you're ready to start your journey!
Is The Meadow free to use?
Yes! The basic membership is completely free and gives you access to our Seed-level tasks. For more advanced tasks and features, you can upgrade to Meadow+ or Steward tiers.

Nectar & Points

How do I earn Nectar points?
You earn Nectar by completing tasks! Each task awards experience points based on its difficulty level (Seed, Sprout, or Bloom). When your experience bar fills to 100, you earn 1 Nectar point. The more consistently you complete tasks, the higher your engagement multiplier becomes.
What can I do with Nectar points?
Nectar points represent your contribution to the Meadow. They unlock milestones and badges, help you climb the ranks, and demonstrate your commitment to sustainability. In the future, Nectar may unlock exclusive rewards and benefits!
What are engagement multipliers?
Engagement multipliers boost the points you earn based on how often you complete tasks within a task group. The more consistent you are, the higher your multiplier (up to 6x with influence). Having referrals also increases your multiplier!

Tasks & Levels

What are the different task stages?
Tasks are organized into three stages:

🌰 Seeds: Entry-level tasks available to everyone
🌱 Sprout: Intermediate tasks requiring Meadow+ membership
🌸 Bloom: Advanced tasks for Steward members

Each stage offers progressively more challenging and impactful tasks.
What's the difference between Novice and Experienced tasks?
Each task has two versions. Novice versions are simpler introductions to the task concept. Once you complete all Novice tasks, you'll be prompted to switch to Experienced versions, which offer more depth and challenge for the same tasks.
What are Sigma Logs?
Sigma Logs are reflection entries using the DMAIC framework (Define, Measure, Analyse, Improve, Control). After completing tasks, you can add reflections to deepen your learning. Standalone Sigma Logs also count towards the Sigma Eye achievement badge.

Account & Membership

What membership tiers are available?
Free: Access to Seed tasks and basic features
Meadow+: Unlocks Sprout tasks and enhanced features
Steward: Full access to all tasks including Bloom level

All tiers include milestones, badges, and community features.
How do I reset my password?
Click "Forgot password?" on the login page and enter your email address. You'll receive a link to reset your password. If you don't see the email, check your spam folder or contact us for help.

Still have questions?

We're here to help. Reach out to our team and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
